TypeScript Almanac

Three streams, distinct dates.

ECMAScript editions, TypeScript releases and runtime availability are separate kinds of event. This catalogue dates each one on its own evidence and links every claim to a primary source. The dated entries run from 2015 to 2026.

Method

How a claim gets in

Every claim on an entry links to the source that establishes it, at the smallest part of that source that states it. Standardization, publication, a stable release and engine availability are recorded as four different facts, and a disagreement between primary sources is kept on the entry rather than settled by preference.
